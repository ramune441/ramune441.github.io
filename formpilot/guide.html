User Guide

Save your profile once, then use AI to fill registration, contact and checkout forms. Free to use, with no account or API key required.

Daily usage limits apply. AI can make mistakes, and some custom form controls are not supported. Always check the results. FormPilot never submits forms for you.

2 · Run AI Auto-Fill

3 · Save & Restore Form Data

Save inputs for a site and restore them on your next visit.

  1. Save Current Form → Save
  2. URL starts with · This entry appears on every page whose URL starts with this text (parameters after "?" are ignored). Shorten it to cover more pages
  3. Restore · Write this saved data back into the form on this page

Up to 20 entries per site and 50 sites in total. Beyond that, the oldest data is deleted automatically.

4 · Keyboard Shortcuts

Press these keys on any page to fill it with this profile

  1. Profile → Shortcut → Change
  2. Press the keys now (Esc to cancel)
  3. Ctrl, Alt or Cmd + any key (e.g. Alt+Shift+1)
  4. Not available: Esc, Tab, and browser keys such as Ctrl+Tab
  5. Clear · Remove this profile's shortcut

Privacy Policy

Saved profile values stay on your device. Form labels, field attributes and custom field names are sent to AI for analysis; your saved profile values are not sent.

AI analysis uses Google Gemini through our Cloudflare Workers proxy. A device identifier and your IP address are used for daily limits and abuse prevention; usage counters and server logs are separate from your saved profiles.

This website uses Google Analytics. Sending the contact form shares your message, optional email and attachments with the developer. The uninstall survey is optional and may include usage diagnostics.

Uninstalling removes the extension’s local profiles, saved forms and settings. It does not automatically delete server usage counters or abuse records.
