Privacy Policy

Saved profile values stay on your device. Form labels, field attributes and custom field names are sent to AI for analysis; your saved profile values are not sent.

AI analysis uses Google Gemini through our Cloudflare Workers proxy. A device identifier and your IP address are used for daily limits and abuse prevention; usage counters and server logs are separate from your saved profiles.

This website uses Google Analytics. Sending the contact form shares your message, optional email and attachments with the developer. The uninstall survey is optional and may include usage diagnostics.

Uninstalling removes the extension’s local profiles, saved forms and settings. It does not automatically delete server usage counters or abuse records.

FAQ

Is FormPilot free? Are there usage limits?

Yes. FormPilot is free to install and use. AI auto-fill has a daily limit per device; when you reach it, the extension shows when the limit resets. Keep up to 10 profiles and 50 custom fields per profile.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. There is no sign-up and no API key to enter. AI requests go through the developer’s proxy server.

What data is sent to the AI?

Saved profile values stay on your device. Form labels, field attributes and custom field names are sent to AI for analysis; your saved profile values are not sent. AI analysis uses Google Gemini through our Cloudflare Workers proxy. A device identifier and your IP address are used for daily limits and abuse prevention; usage counters and server logs are separate from your saved profiles.

Which forms does FormPilot work on?

Registration, contact, checkout and other web forms that use text fields, select menus, radio buttons, checkboxes and date fields, including supported embedded forms. AI can make mistakes and some custom form controls are not supported, so always check the results.

Does FormPilot submit forms automatically?

No. FormPilot only fills in the fields and never clicks the submit button. Check the filled values, then submit the form yourself.

Which languages and countries are supported?

Use the interface in 20 languages, with names, phone numbers, postcodes and dates adapted to supported field instructions. Conversions follow supported field instructions: Japanese kana, Thai Buddhist years, Taiwan ROC years, Dutch surname prefixes and Russian patronymics. Supply identity numbers, other people’s names and unsupported calendars as custom fields; these are never invented.

How do I edit or switch profiles?

Open the FormPilot popup, go to the “Profile” tab, change the fields and click “Save”. To fill with a different profile, choose it under “Active Profile”.

Does it work on mobile?

As a Chrome extension, it requires the desktop version of Google Chrome. It is not available on mobile Chrome.
