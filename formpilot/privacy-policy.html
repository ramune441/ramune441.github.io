Privacy Policy

Saved profile values stay on your device. Form labels, field attributes and custom field names are sent to AI for analysis; your saved profile values are not sent.

AI analysis uses Google Gemini through our Cloudflare Workers proxy. A device identifier and your IP address are used for daily limits and abuse prevention; usage counters and server logs are separate from your saved profiles.

This website uses Google Analytics. Sending the contact form shares your message, optional email and attachments with the developer. The uninstall survey is optional and may include usage diagnostics.

Uninstalling removes the extension’s local profiles, saved forms and settings. It does not automatically delete server usage counters or abuse records.
