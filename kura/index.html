Cleanup

Swipe to clean up,
a whole batch at once.

Kura finds exact duplicates, similar photos, blurry shots and large files on your phone. Swipe right to keep, left to trash, and see right away how much space you can free.

  • The best shot is marked among similar photos
  • Clean up by month or by folder
  • Undo any step; trashed photos wait in the trash
Edit

Better photos,
and a better you.

Retouch, reshape, makeup, hair and background, plus 30 filters, adjustments and crop. There are filters that make dishes look delicious and body tools for your whole figure. You always save a copy, so the original stays as it was.

  • AI edits for hairstyle and expression (with a cloud plan)
  • "Ask AI": describe the change you want
  • Edits are saved separately; the original stays
Library

The photo you want,
found in seconds.

A grid by date, with memories from the same day in past years. Search by place, by what is in the photo or by text inside it, and faces are grouped by person on your phone.

  • People, places (map), albums and folders
  • Motion photos, panoramas and slideshows
  • Lossless JPEG rotation and printing
Private

Photos you'd rather not show,
kept private.

Move photos and videos to Private to hide them from your gallery and other apps. Open it with your phone's screen lock.

  • Lock the whole app if you like
  • Location is removed from shared photos (on by default)
Backup

Original quality,
safe in the cloud.

Back up photos and videos with no recompression. Remove backed-up copies from your phone to free up space; they stay viewable from the cloud.

  • Monthly plans: 100 GB / 500 GB / 1 TB
  • Share with a link, with an expiry date and password
  • View and organize from your computer's browser too
Move

Move from Google Photos,
all at once.

Just send your Google Takeout .zip from the app or the web app. Kura brings over your albums, favorites and the photos it doesn't have yet. Kura's server unpacks it in the background, so you can close the app and the browser.

  • Moving needs a cloud plan
  • For large libraries, the web app on a computer is easiest
  • Locations recorded by your camera stay in the files
Web

The same library in your computer's browser.

Photos in your Kura cloud can be viewed, organized, cleaned up, edited, shared and uploaded in the web app, just like in the app. Favorites stay in sync with the app.

Privacy

Your photos are sorted without leaving your phone.

Photos leave your phone only when you do something that clearly sends them somewhere.

All detection on your phone

Duplicate, blur, face, object and text detection run on your phone. No photo is uploaded to be sorted.

Always asked before deleting

Android asks you before any file is deleted, and trashed photos wait in the trash until you empty it.

Only your account ID is sent

When you sign in, our server keeps only your Google account's ID. Your name and email address are never sent.

AI edits send only the face area

AI edits send only the area around the face, and neither the photo nor the result is kept on the server.

Before you start
