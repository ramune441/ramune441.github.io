Dual subtitles
Two subtitle tracks, one box. The top line is subtitle 1, the bottom line is subtitle 2 — merged into a single frame with no gap.
Pick languages
Pick languages in the popup. Every track the title offers is listed, and the badge shows which site you're setting up — each one keeps its own settings.
Adjust on screen
Adjust it right on the video. Click to select, drag to move, scroll to resize a line, arrow keys to nudge, one click to align left / center / right.

Features

Small extension, thought through

📺
One extension, four services
Prime Video, YouTube, Netflix and Disney+ — the same two-line subtitles and the same controls, everywhere you watch.
🎬
Any two of the title's languages
Every subtitle track the site provides is detected the moment the video plays — on YouTube, auto-generated subtitles are offered too. Pick one per line, or hide one side.
🖱️
Adjust on the video
Drag to move, scroll to resize the line under the pointer, arrow keys to nudge, align left, center or right from the toolbar. It scales with fullscreen and steps aside when the control bar appears.
🎨
Style each line
Text color, text transparency and background transparency, set separately for the top and bottom lines. Both always share one clean box.
☁️
Each site remembers its own setup
Big subtitles on YouTube, subtle ones on Netflix — settings are saved per service and synced through your Chrome, so they follow you to any device.
🛡️
🔒 100% local
SubDuo has no server. It only reads the subtitle files each site already sends to your browser for the title you're watching.
