Focus & Earn

Focus, and
free time “ripens.”

In windows with the presence check on, the focus timer only advances while your face is in front of the screen. Finish the goal and you earn the minutes you set for social or video apps. Rounds you quit early earn nothing.

  • A countdown that runs only while you’re present (brief glances away are allowed)
  • Harvest “usable time” when you finish (none if you quit or stop early)
  • Set focus length (5–120 min), minutes earned and rounds

Focus windows

Your own rules,
for each time of day.

Just paint the weekly grid to set the days and hours you want to focus. Each window has its own focus goal, minutes per round, round cap, blocked apps and camera presence check. Outside the windows, you’re free.

  • Paint the grid to set days and times in one go
  • Pick blocked apps by category for each window (with each app’s usage time)
  • Optional “pre-leisure” minutes you can use first
Gentle lock

At zero,
it locks gently.

Once you’ve used up a window’s time, opening a chosen app shows a block screen. Distracting apps are locked during focus sessions too. A “settings lock” stops you from loosening the rules later.

  • Target apps are blocked automatically at 0 minutes left
  • Earn time right from the block screen by focusing or with a word quiz
  • Settings lock prevents “I’ll loosen it later”
Presence check

“Pretending to focus”
doesn’t work here.

A timer alone keeps counting even when you walk away. Ripen uses the front camera to judge only whether a face is in front of the screen — on your device. No image is saved or sent. You can also choose windows without the camera.

  • On-device face detection with Google ML Kit
  • No image or video is saved or sent (only seconds are logged)
  • Turn it off per window to focus with the timer alone

Privacy & Safety

A camera — and still peace of mind.

“A camera to check focus” can sound worrying. Ripen is designed privacy-first.

🔒Processed entirely on-device

Face presence detection runs on your device with Google ML Kit. Video is never sent to an external server.

🚫No images saved or sent

It only judges whether a face is there. Photos and video are never saved or sent. All it logs is how many seconds you were present and focused.

📱Data stays on your device

Focus records and settings are stored on your device and never sent to Ripen’s servers. If Android backup is on, your settings are included in your Google Account backup and restored when you reinstall.

🔑No account, no ads

No sign-up or login, and no ads or analytics tools inside. Download it and start right away.

⚠️Before you start

  • Blocking apps requires the “Usage access” and “Accessibility” permissions. Ripen won’t open its home screen until both are on (Android requires you to enable them manually in Settings).
  • The camera permission is requested the first time you focus in a window with the presence check on. Windows with it turned off, and focus outside your windows, run on the timer alone without the camera.
  • It doesn’t make apps completely unusable — it’s a “soft” system designed to change your behavior. Outside your windows, blocked apps are free to use.
  • Face detection uses the front camera, so a well-lit place is recommended.
  • The app is available in 12 languages: Japanese, English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, Russian, German, French, Korean and Italian.
