Features

Features

AI transforms and snippets — two engines to write, fix, and reply faster.

🤖

AI presets

Comes with Polish, Translate, Summarize, and Fix typos. Select and choose, or type a trigger like //p + Enter. Add up to 10 of your own (instructions up to 200 characters).

⚡

Snippets

Type a trigger like ;sig and confirm to instantly expand your saved text. No AI, unlimited uses. Up to 1,000 characters, 10 snippets.

↩️

Write a reply

Select a message you received and AI drafts a reply. Pick a tone, add a quick hint, then copy it wherever you need.

Selection toolbar, right-click menu, or shortcut (Alt+Shift+S). In a field, a trigger alone works too. Never breaks your flow.

📝

Any text field

Email, chat, social, docs — anywhere you can type. Polished text drops right back into the field. Read-only text is copied automatically.

🌏

Privacy & Safety

Only the text you pick ever leaves your browser.

Built privacy-first. It never reads your whole page.

🔒 Settings stay local

The presets and settings you create are stored only inside your browser — never collected on an external server.

🤖 Only the target text goes to AI

Only the text you select or type is sent to AI for transforming. The rest of the page and your history are never sent.

🛡️ Minimal permissions

Only the permissions needed to read/write text and reach the AI proxy. No camera, microphone, or location access.

🎉 Free, no sign-up

No account or API key. AI runs are free up to 50/day, and snippet expansion is unlimited.

FAQ
