How to use

No more copy-paste round-trips. Done in 4 steps.

No pasting into another AI tab — it all happens right in your field.

1
✏️

Select or type

Highlight the text, or type a trigger in the field

2
✦

Launch it

Via the selection toolbar, right-click, or a shortcut

3
🎚️

AI transforms or expands

Polish, translate, summarize, reply — or expand a snippet

4
✅

Apply it

One tap drops it into the field — read-only text is copied

🤖Transform with AI

1. Select the text
2. Click the ✦ icon that appears
3. Pick a style (or type your own)
4. Press Transform
5. Apply to swap it in

⚡Insert a snippet

1. In a field, type a snippet trigger (e.g. ;sig)
2. Press space
3. It instantly expands to your saved text

↩️Write a reply

1. Select the received message → click ✦
2. Check ↩ Draft a reply
3. Pick a tone, type what you want to say
4. Generate reply → Copy
Launch

Several ways to launch

✦ Selection toolbar — select text and click the icon that appears.
🖱️ Right-click menu — right-click your selection → SendReady → a preset.
⌨️ Shortcut — press Alt+Shift+S to open the palette.
✦ In a field — click a text field to show the icon and insert a snippet.
Triggers

Keyboard triggers (set a style, too)

🪄 AI trigger — in a field, type a trigger then your text, e.g. //p your text, and press Enter. The trigger picks the style (//p = polite, //en = translate, //sum = summarize…), so you transform right where you type — no menu needed.
⚡ Snippet trigger — type ;sig and press space to expand a saved snippet.
