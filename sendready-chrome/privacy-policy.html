SendReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how the developer Ramune (the "Developer") handles information in the Chrome extension "SendReady" (the "Extension"). The Extension uses AI to rewrite, translate, summarize or reply to text you select or type, and inserts snippets you have saved.

1. Information stored in your browser

The Extension stores the following with the Chrome Storage API. The Developer cannot see it.

  • Settings (chrome.storage.sync): your presets and snippets, theme, display language and toolbar setting. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs them across your signed-in browsers through your Google account.
  • Device data (chrome.storage.local): a random device identifier (deviceId, in the form "chrome:" + UUID), your remaining AI uses for the day, your last 5 AI results (history), the review-prompt status and number of successful uses, whether the how-to page has been shown, the install date and the last error code.

Expanding a snippet happens entirely in your browser and sends nothing.

2. Information sent when you run an AI action

Only when you run an AI action (polish, translate, summarize, fix, a custom instruction or a reply), the Extension sends the following to an AI proxy the Developer runs on Cloudflare Workers (https://sendready-1-22.warload57.workers.dev). The proxy passes the text to Google Gemini and returns the result.

  • the text you selected or typed as the target (up to 1,000 characters)
  • the style or instruction you chose, and for a reply, the reply content you entered
  • your deviceId

The Extension does not read or send the rest of the page, your browsing history or other personal information. As with any connection, the proxy sees your IP address.

3. What the AI proxy keeps

The proxy does not store your text or the AI result. To enforce the limit of 50 AI uses per day and to prevent abuse, it keeps a usage count per deviceId for 24 hours. If abuse is detected (for example, a burst of requests or far more than the daily limit), the deviceId is added to a block list with no expiry date, and if several blocked devices share an IP address, that IP address is added too.

4. Third parties

The Developer does not sell your information or share it with anyone other than the providers needed to run the AI feature: Cloudflare (hosting the proxy) and Google (Gemini API). For how Google handles the text, see the Gemini API Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The Extension contains no ads, tracking or analytics.

5. This website, the contact form and the uninstall survey

This website (not the Extension) uses Google Analytics to count page visits. If you send the contact form or the uninstall survey, we receive what you enter (including your email address and attachment, if you add them) and the anonymous diagnostics the Extension adds to the link: version, display language, days since install, number of successful AI uses and last error code. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the Extension.

6. Permissions

The Extension requests only the following. It does not request access to your camera, microphone or location.

  • storage: saving presets, settings, history and the deviceId
  • contextMenus: opening SendReady from the right-click menu
  • clipboardWrite: copying results to the clipboard
  • Content script on web pages: showing the SendReady button next to text you select or the field you type in, and inserting the result. It reads only the text you select or type, and sends it only when you run an AI action.
  • Host permission for the AI proxy: connecting to the proxy above

7. Deleting your data

You can clear the history in the Extension's popup. Uninstalling the Extension deletes everything it stored in your browser. The proxy's usage count expires after 24 hours. For block-list records or messages you sent us, contact us below.
